          About DesignYourTaol

          A Digital, Interactive Way to Personalize a Difficult Decision

          • Addresses limited customization and difficulty visualizing the final design
          • Reduces miscommunication between families and staff
          • Cuts down manual processing, design errors and repeated revisions
